Local Bluey

A desktop pet that lives on your Mac, talks, and - with your explicit approval - drives your computer. Your voice and screen stay yours.

What it does

  • Wake and talk. Say the wake word or hold to talk; Bluey answers with a voice and a face, not a chat window.
  • Safe computer control. Bluey can click, type and open apps - but every risky action asks you first, and a kill switch stops everything instantly.
  • Phone remote. Pair an iPhone over your local network to wake the Mac, talk to it, or put it to sleep.
  • Point at things. Ask about what's on screen; screenshots are withheld entirely in local-only mode.

Privacy, stated plainly

  • Local-only mode talks to a model on your own machine (Ollama) and nothing else - verified by a strict loopback check.
  • Every outbound connection is recorded in an on-device egress log you can read: what left, where to, how big.
  • Voice recordings are deleted right after transcription.
  • Known gaps are tracked as open issues, not hidden: issue tracker.

Platform status

  • macOS: primary target, works today.
  • iOS: the phone remote, works today.
  • Android and Linux: planned (#51-#54).

Try it

Flutter 3.47.6, then flutter run -d macos. Full setup: the repository README.
