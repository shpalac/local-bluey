# Demo script (60-90 s)

Record on a clean demo account or a fresh user window. Never show real
screens with personal data.

1. **Idle face (5 s).** Bluey sits on screen, blinking, following the
   cursor. "This is Bluey. It lives on my Mac."
2. **Wake and small talk (10 s).** Say the wake word, then "Hey Bluey,
   what can you do?" Bluey answers out loud with its face talking.
3. **Hold to talk (10 s).** Hold the hotkey: "What time is it in
   Tokyo?" Release; the answer is spoken.
4. **Safe computer control (25 s).** "Open Notes." The safety-gate
   dialog appears: "Open app 'Notes'?" Approve it. Notes opens. Then
   "type: demo notes from Bluey" - the confirmation shows the exact
   text before typing. Point out: nothing risky runs without a yes.
5. **Kill switch (10 s).** Trigger it; everything stops. "One click
   cancels anything in flight."
6. **Phone remote (15 s).** Show the paired iPhone: wake the Mac from
   the phone, hold to talk remotely.
7. **Privacy close (10 s).** Open the egress log: "Everything that
   left this Mac is listed here. In local-only mode, nothing leaves."

Do not show: the API key field filled in, real documents, real
notifications, or your actual desktop wallpaper clutter.
