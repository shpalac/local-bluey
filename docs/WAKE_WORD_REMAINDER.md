## Current answer

The dormant service and its injected lifecycle guarantees are delivered. They are not an activated on-device wake-word feature. [WakeWordService](../lib/services/wake_word.dart) accepts a nullable spotter; no engine is bundled. With no engine, `start` returns before preference/permission/capture. Its preference defaults off. An all-lib search found no production construction, start/stop caller, spotter implementation, onWake registration or listening/error binding. [SettingsScreen](../lib/ui/settings_screen.dart) has no wake-word preference tile or service writer. [DataRegistry](../lib/services/data_registry.dart) describes and deletes the stored toggle only; deletion does not stop a running injected service.

[Main](../lib/main.dart) and [FaceScreen](../lib/ui/face_screen.dart) use `onWakeChanged(bool)` for manual face wake/sleep, not this service's `onWake()` callback. The service comments claiming main hookup, a bound mic indicator and overlapping windows are not proof: the actual loop is sequential start, two-second delay, stop, score, optional confirmation, then next window. No overlapping-window ring buffer is implemented. [README](../README.md) still marks the real spotter unshipped and describes manual wake. No source/comment/history was changed by this audit.

## What the current fixtures establish

Full [wake tests](../test/wake_word_test.dart) were read, including fake driver/spotter/transcriber helpers and teardown. The 24 cases cover score-only dormancy/threshold/remote configuration/default, actual service capture start/window/score/confirmation/settings races, explicit retry, old/new generation, permission staleness, exact failed-stop candidate retention and restart. Fake recorder writes three bytes, gates entered stages and tracks open counts. Score mocks return numbers, not recognized audio.

PR402's start-failure fixtures deliberately see lastError while cleanup and listening remain pending, then hold/release actual driver stop and join service.stop before exact file/state assertions. The window-delay failure has the opposite error-publication order: cleanup entry precedes the error. Teardown releases gates and joins actual service/capture before deleting isolated files. Adjacent score/transcriber/failed-stop fixtures also join cleanup. These are delivered observation fixes, not extra production guarantees.

[AudioCapture](../lib/services/audio_capture.dart), [capture tests](../test/audio_capture_test.dart), [identity tests](../test/audio_capture_identity_test.dart) and [permission tests](../test/audio_capture_permission_test.dart) provide distinct token/legacy capture contracts. Wake still uses the legacy path; token tests do not imply its adoption. [Privacy tests](../test/privacy_guard_test.dart), [STT tests](../test/stt_test.dart), [deadline tests](../test/stt_deadline_test.dart) and [egress tests](../test/egress_monitor_test.dart) establish their own injected boundaries, not a running microphone or engine.
